---

---

## Design Notes

- Responsive layout for mobile, tablet and desktop.
- Sage green, warm ivory and walnut brown brand palette.
- Clear navigation and readable expense information.
- Consistent buttons, cards and spacing.
- Separate views for groups, expenses and settlements.

**Note:** All names and amounts shown are fictional examples for the wireframes.
